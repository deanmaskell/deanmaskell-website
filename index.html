Does This Sound Familiar?

You built something really good. The business was profitable and growing. Then over time:

  • Revenue has slowed down
  • The cost to get new customers has crept up
  • The cost of running the business has kept rising

You know something needs to change. You want real, tangible commercial outcomes. But you are carrying the problem alone and you don't know where to turn.

What do you do next?

The answers are not obvious. And without the right experience, you can spend a lot of money making things worse.

  1. When revenue slows, someone suggests more advertising. A new Google Ads campaign. A second channel. But nobody has asked why revenue slowed in the first place.
  2. When the cost to get new customers creeps up, someone tries to fix it. They cut the bids and tighten the targeting. The cost per acquisition comes down. But so does volume. And revenue falls further.
  3. When the cost of running the business keeps rising, someone discovers AI. Hand it to a junior person. Cut the team. But AI without proper oversight does not save money. It just moves the cost somewhere harder to see.

It is always the same. A solution gets chosen before the problem gets understood. And that is a costly mistake.

What Changes?

I start by understanding why revenue has slowed, why customer acquisition is getting more expensive, and why the business costs more to run. Then I work on all three.

Increase revenue from the right customers

  • Know who your best customers actually are.
  • Get more from the customers you already have.
  • Find more customers who look like your best ones.

Lower cost to get those customers

  • Know what each channel is actually costing you.
  • Stop spending on what is not working.
  • Double down on what is.

Lower cost of running the business

  • Everyone working from the same numbers.
  • Agencies and teams with clear accountability.
  • AI and systems that reduce the cost of doing business over time.

Why Fractional

Hiring a senior growth or marketing director takes time you probably don't have. The recruitment process alone typically runs three to six months. A senior commercial or marketing director at a business your size, outside London, will cost £80,000 to £110,000 in base salary. Add a recruitment fee of 20–25%, employer National Insurance at 13.8%, and pension and benefits. The true cost in year one is well over £130,000. If it doesn't work out, you start again.

Fractional removes that risk entirely. No recruitment process, no long-term employment commitment, senior experience from day one. A retained engagement of two days a month costs £20,400 a year. That is roughly one sixth of the true cost of a permanent hire, with none of the downside. The starting point is a two-day diagnostic at £1,700. If it's useful, we agree what ongoing involvement looks like. If it isn't, you've spent two days and a fraction of what a wrong hire costs, without the months it took to find them.
